# Alpha District — Brand Profile

> Source of truth for the auto-posting agent. Every generated caption + image references this file.

## What it is
Premium **gym-to-street "Pump Cover" apparel**. Heavyweight 300GSM oversized cotton tees (£35) and hoodies (£55), customised in-store via instant **DTG printing** (10–12 min). London flagship. Private Ltd.

## Mission & philosophy
**"Freedom vs Constraint"** — the transformation from generic, mass-produced gym wear into expressive, identity-driven apparel. Empower people to express mental strength, discipline, and personal philosophy through what they wear.

## Audience
Gen Z **males 18–25** in London. Gym-goers, bodybuilding & powerlifting "tribes," anime/manga fans, stoicism & self-improvement crowd. Native to TikTok & Instagram. Value individuality, authenticity, community over mass-market logos.

## Design DNA / themes
1. **Manga & anime** artwork
2. **Stoic philosophy** (discipline, control, mindset)
3. **Powerlifting / motivational** imagery

## Tone of voice
Bold · motivational · aspirational · raw & authentic · disciplined "alpha" energy with a stoic/philosophical edge. Community-first, never corporate. Avoid: toxic masculinity, hype-bro clichés, generic fitness platitudes.

## Visual identity
- **Mood:** dark, premium, tactical/stoic, gritty gym aesthetic, oversized silhouettes, manga linework. Military-grade restraint over hype.
- **Palette:** Onyx `#0E0F0D` · Dark Olive `#2E3622` · Olive `#4B5A33` · Steel `#98A0A6` · Bone `#F4F4F1`. (Olive = identity/primary, Steel = secondary/UI, Bone = text/highlights, Onyx = base.)
- **Logo:** "AD" monogram in Bone on an Olive rounded-square badge (primary). Wordmark "ALPHA DISTRICT" in Anton all-caps. Files in `assets/`.
- **Fonts (free Google Fonts):** Display = **Anton** (condensed, bold, all-caps headlines/logo); Body = **Inter** (captions, clean). Fully tonal — no extra accent color.
